# Ruko: 60-second demo video

**Goal:** one real scam call, caught live, with the warning heard on camera. This is aimed at the Demo Day Drop award and gives your post something to share.

**Rules:** everything shown must really work. Use only synthetic scripts, fake names and fake numbers. Show one honest limitation.

## Setup
- **Phone A** (the "victim"): an old keypad or basic phone if you have one, on speaker. A grandparent's phone looks best, used with their consent.
- **Phone B:** your teammate plays the scammer and calls Phone A, reading the PM-Kisan script from `backend/scripts.py`.
- **Ruko:** running on a third phone or laptop next to Phone A at the deployed URL. Warning language: **Kannada** (or your own language).
- **Recording:** use one camera for the room. Separately, screen-record Ruko's screen. Use the room audio so the spoken warning is heard.

## Tips
- Record the call in a quiet room first, then a second take with a TV on, to show it copes with noise (for the Noise Canceller award).
- Keep subtitles large, because most people watch with the sound off.
- Export 1080×1920 (vertical) for LinkedIn and X, under 60 s.
