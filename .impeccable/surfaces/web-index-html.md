## Scope
Landing page `web/index.html` (Persuade). The guard app `web/app/` and family screen `web/family/` (Operate) inherit the same world in a quieter register.

## Audience and action
Adult child (25–40) protecting a parent. Primary action: "Protect my parents", which opens the app's Family Shield setup. Proof: measured results in docs/EVALUATION.md; solo build; no users or testimonials exist, so none are shown.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A warning sign tells you what is ahead before you reach it. Ruko is that sign for the scammer's next move. Refuses the category default: a dark shield-and-padlock security hero, or a gradient SaaS split hero with icon cards.
OWN-WORLD: Asphalt ground (#26282b) with retroreflective sign panels: sign white, cautionary red border (#d0202e) used only for what is happening now, hazard yellow (#ffc20e) for "ahead". Hazard chevrons as section dividers. Overpass (Highway Gothic lineage) for Latin and Mukta for Devanagari, self-hosted. Square-cornered sign panels with a thin inner border. No gradients, glass or soft shadows.
STORY: The visitor sees a real scam call walk past four warning signs; understands that Ruko spots the scam playbook and warns before the next step, on any phone, with the family alerted; believes it from measured numbers; taps "Protect my parents".
