In short:
  • Ruko is a warning tool. It can miss a scam or warn about a genuine call.
  • It is not a replacement for 1930, the police or your bank.
  • Use it only on calls you're entitled to protect.

1. What Ruko is

Ruko listens to a phone call, recognises the common steps of phone scams, and warns out loud. It can also alert a family member and prepare a complaint for India's cyber-fraud helpline 1930.

Ruko only warns. It does not block, end or record calls, and it never takes any action on your bank account.

2. No guarantee

Scammers change their words, calls can be noisy, and speech recognition makes mistakes. Ruko may:

  • miss a scam;
  • warn late;
  • warn about a genuine call.

Ruko is offered as it is, while it is being tested and improved. Always use your own judgement: never share an OTP, PIN or password, and never send money because of a phone call.

If money has left your account, call 1930 immediately and contact your bank.

3. Who can use Ruko

  • You must be 18 or older to create an account.
  • You may protect yourself.
  • You may protect a family member who has agreed to it.
