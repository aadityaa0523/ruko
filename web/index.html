      Scammers change numbers every day. They never change the script.

      Caller ID and spam lists check the number. Ruko listens to what is being said. Every phone scam walks through the same four signs, in every language, and Ruko knows which sign comes next.

      1. Pretends to be someone you trust

        “CBI officer bol raha hoon” · “SBI head office se”

        Ruko warns: next they will ask for an OTP.

      2. Scares you or rushes you

        “Warrant nikla hai” · “aaj raat bijli kat jayegi”

        Ruko warns: real police never arrest anyone on a call.

      3. Cuts you off from family

        “Kisi ko mat batana, phone mat kaatna”

        Ruko warns: tell your family now. Your family's phone is already red.

      4. Asks for OTP, money or your phone

        “OTP bata dijiye” · “safe account mein transfer” · “AnyDesk”

        Ruko warns: cut the call now and call 1930.

      Tested on noisy phone audio, not just in a quiet room.

      36 live runs through Gnani's speech models: 7 scam calls in 5 languages and 2 genuine calls, on a speakerphone and on an 8 kHz phone line, with and without a TV-like voice in the background. All calls are scripted and spoken by synthetic voices.

      Where it struggles: with background chatter, 2 of 14 alerts came about 4.5 seconds late. Ruko only warns; it never blocks a call.
