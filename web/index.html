      Why caller ID isn't enough

      Scammers change numbers every day. They don't change the script.

      Spam lists block known numbers. A new SIM gets past them. But every phone scam follows the same four steps, whatever language it's in. Ruko tracks the steps, so it knows how far a call has gone and warns about the next move before it comes.

      1. Pretends to be someone you trust“CBI officer”, “bank head office”, “PM-Kisan office”, “I sent money by mistake”
      2. Scares you or rushes youArrest warrant, account freeze, electricity cut tonight, last date today
      3. Cuts you off from family“Don't tell anyone, don't cut the call”
      4. Asks for OTP, money or your phoneOTP, UPI PIN, “safe account”, AnyDesk, a link

      Family Shield

      Scams work when people are alone. So Ruko calls in the family.

      Parents send one link to their children. The moment a call turns into a scam, the child's phone turns red with live captions and tells them what to do: call now, because a second call breaks the scammer's hold.

      • Live captions and scam steps on the family member's phone
      • Keypad phones: merge the call with Ruko's number and Ruko warns inside the call
      • Nobody sees anything unless a call is being protected
